# Vibe profile — "Telugu Feel-Good Upbeat"

A reusable curation profile for Telugu mixes in the **feel-good upbeat / pop-rock / energetic-romantic** lane. Sister profile to `vibe-telugu-9xm-feels.md` (which is the chill cousin).

Use this when the user asks for: "Telugu feel-good," "upbeat Telugu," "Telugu pop songs," "happy Telugu mix," "road trip Telugu," "college energy Telugu," or names tracks like Happy (2006) — Happy / Happy Days — Oh My Friend / Bommarillu — Appudo Ippudo / Crazy Feeling.

## The mood in one paragraph

Bright, energetic, peppy. College canteen at noon, scooter ride with friends, road trip out of the city. Mid-to-uptempo (~110–140 BPM), often guitar-and-drums driven, sometimes synth-pop. Lyrics are hopeful / playful / "let's go" rather than longing or grieving. The crucial distinction from the chill lane: **upbeat-feel-good moves your shoulders**; **chill-warm-romantic doesn't**.

This is *not* mass / item / hero-entry territory. The user has been clear: "feel-good upbeat and pop" means **musical, melodic, hooky** — not Pushpa-style mass anthems, not item songs, not Naatu-Naatu folk-energy. It's the youth-romance / college / road-trip register.

## What's IN — track shape

- **Mid to uptempo** — roughly 110–140 BPM as a mood guide. Anything that makes your head bob.
- **Singer-led pop or pop-rock production**. Karthik (the upbeat side — "Crazy Feeling," "Edo Priyaragam"), Anurag Kulkarni (the upbeat side — "Ramuloo Ramulaa," "Ranjithame"), Anirudh Ravichander as singer/composer (he defines this lane post-2020), Hesham Abdul Wahab, Yuvan Shankar Raja's pop side, GV Prakash on Telugu, Vivek-Mervin.
- **Coming-of-age / friendship / road / "first job, first crush" films** — Happy (2006), Happy Days (2007), Bommarillu (2006), Bhale Bhale Magadivoi (2015), Pelli Choopulu (2016), Nenu Sailaja (2016), Geetha Govindam (2018) upbeat tracks, Andhra King Taluka (2025).
- **Famous, mainstream-film catalog** — same rule as the chill profile. In Telugu the genre lives inside film.
- **Anirudh-coded modern bangers that are still musical, not mass**. Hoyna Hoyna, Baitikochi Chuste, Kollagottey, Gundellonaa, Sa Ri Ma Pa.

## What's OUT — and why

- **Item songs / cabaret**. Saami Saami, Oo Antava, Butta Bomma, Daakko Daakko Meka, item-track inserts. They live in a different lane (mass-dance / club).
- **Mass hero-entry anthems**. Srivalli (Allu Arjun mass-stomp), Naatu Naatu (folk-mass), Nannaku Prematho title (NTR mass intro). Wrong vibe — these are made for screen-tear theater claps, not for personal listening.
- **Heartbreak / grief ballads**. Channa-Mereya-equivalent. This is the *upbeat* lane — sad doesn't fit.
- **Pure devotional / patriotic**. Different lane.
- **The chill warm-romantic lane** (Inkem Inkem, Yedo Priyaragam, Em Sandeham Ledu, Aakasam Ammayithe). Those go in the `9xm-feels` profile, not here. There's overlap at the seam — songs like Crazy Feeling, Motta Modatisari, Jatha Kalise, Ay Pilla sit on the boundary and could go either way.
- **Pushpa-2 / Pushpa Saami-Saami / mass-folk**. Mostly out.

## Singer / composer palette

**Modern (2018+)**: Anirudh Ravichander (as singer & composer — he's basically the genre's anchor right now), Anurag Kulkarni's upbeat side, Hesham Abdul Wahab, Vivek-Mervin, GV Prakash, Pawan Ch, Ram Miriyala (the pop side, not the folk-mass side). Sid Sriram occasionally — but he's mostly chill, so cap at 1–2.

**Older (2001–2015)**: Yuvan Shankar Raja Telugu (Happy 2006), Mickey J Meyer (the upbeat tracks from Happy Days / KBL), Devi Sri Prasad (his pop-rock side — Bommarillu's "Appudo Ippudo," not his mass-DSP side), AR Rahman Telugu pop (Roja, Saahasam Swaasaga Saagipo). Karthik vocals across the era.

**Composer signal — wrong lane**: Thaman's mass-anthem side, DSP's mass-hero side, Bheems Ceciroleo (mostly folk-mass), Mani Sharma's mass-romantic uptempo, M.M. Keeravani anything devotional/period.

## Era weighting

Modern-skewed by default. The upbeat-pop lane has gotten *more* defined post-2018 with Anirudh-Telugu, Geetha Govindam-era, Hi Nanna-era, Andhra King Taluka. So:

- **8–10 tracks from 2018–2025** (Anirudh tracks, Hi Nanna upbeat, Geetha Govindam upbeat, Andhra King Taluka, Pushpa non-mass pop)
- **5–7 tracks from 2010–2017** (Bhale Bhale Magadivoi, Pelli Choopulu, Nenu Sailaja, Karthikeya, Saahasam Swaasaga Saagipo, S/O Satyamurthy upbeat side)
- **3–5 tracks from 2001–2009** (Happy 2006, Happy Days 2007 upbeat side, Bommarillu, Kushi 2001 upbeat, Aarya, Pokiri non-mass)

## Source playlists / jukeboxes for harvesting

**Anchor source**: Spotify's editorial *"Happy Vibes Telugu!"* (50 tracks, 163K saves). This is curated by Spotify's Indian editorial team and is the closest thing to a canonical reference for this lane.

**Per-album jukeboxes**: Happy 2006, Happy Days 2007, Bommarillu, Bhale Bhale Magadivoi, Pelli Choopulu, Nenu Sailaja, Karthikeya, Geetha Govindam, Andhra King Taluka, Saripodhaa Sanivaaram, Hi Nanna, Robinhood. These are the album-level sources where the upbeat tracks live.

**Avoid for harvesting**: "Telugu Mass Beats" / "Pakka Beat" / "Telugu Party Songs" jukeboxes — those are the mass / item / hero-entry lane, not feel-good upbeat. They'll contaminate the harvest.
